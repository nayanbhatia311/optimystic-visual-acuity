# Privacy

Optimystic is designed to work without an online account or cloud service.

## Data handled by the app

The app may handle visual-acuity test settings, responses, results, session history, and a locally generated device identity used for nearby pairing. This information is stored on the user's device. The app does not include advertising, analytics, or a remote data-collection endpoint.

## Local network

When the two-device workflow is used, Optimystic requests local-network access to discover and communicate with the other device. Traffic remains on the local network. Users should pair only with a device they recognize and control.

## Sharing

Results leave the device only when a user deliberately uses an export or share action. The destination selected in Apple's share sheet is controlled by the user and may have its own privacy policy.

## Deleting data

Users can delete saved sessions in the app. Removing the app also removes its locally stored app data, subject to the device's backup and restoration settings.

## Medical notice

Optimystic is an educational and screening aid, not a medical device. It is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent disease. Seek advice from a qualified eye-care professional for medical concerns.

## Questions

Open a GitHub issue for privacy questions or to report an unexpected data flow.
